OVOS Start launcher

This launcher reads the short setup code from the OVOS Start wizard and prepares your installer.

Copy the complete command from your finished wizard, then paste it in a terminal on the device where OVOS will run. A 64-bit operating system is required.

Codes expire one hour after creation. If yours expires, reopen the wizard and choose “Copy new command”. Your choices are kept.
